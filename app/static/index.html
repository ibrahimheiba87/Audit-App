Users

Preparers upload files, run checks, comparisons and samples, add findings, notes and evidence, map accounts, post proposed adjusting entries, sign off sections as prepared and reply to review notes. Reviewers can also clear and reopen findings, change severity, remove evidence and files, post booked entries, mark proposed ones booked, delete adjusting entries, raise and clear review notes, sign off sections as reviewed, and add or edit clients and engagements. Partners can also set materiality, sign off completion and delete clients and engagements. Admins can also manage users and see the whole activity log. Client users belong to one client and only see that client's document requests, where they upload files and message the team.
